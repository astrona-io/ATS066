# Summary

DevOps connects development and operation through shared goals, small changes and useful feedback. DevSecOps integrates security responsibility into that lifecycle rather than adding one final check. Platform teams enable repeated capabilities through supported interfaces, while application teams retain their domain decisions. Clear service boundaries, ownership and feedback prevent automation from becoming a new silo that hides failures or shifts unexplained work onto users.
